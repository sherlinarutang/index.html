De Bono's 6 Thinking Hats Reference

Communication Skills Framework

White Hat: Information & Data

Focuses purely on verified facts, available data, statistics, and objective findings without spin.

Red Hat: Emotions & Intuition

Expresses gut feelings, emotional reactions, empathy, and intuitive impressions without needing logical proof.

Black Hat: Caution & Critical Risk

Identifies potential legal threats, brand risks, flaws, hazards, and why a communication plan might fail.

Yellow Hat: Optimism & Benefits

Highlights value propositions, positive outcomes, strategic advantages, and campaign gains.

Green Hat: Creativity & Alternatives

Lateral thinking, novel campaign formats, out-of-the-box ideas, and unconventional media solutions.

Blue Hat: Process & Control

Meta-thinking, chairing team meetings, setting agendas, synthesizing views, and enforcing deadlines.
